Three small steps.
One lighter inbox.

For the things you need now, without turning them into things you keep forever.

01 / CAPTURE IT

A place for passing things.

Save text, web links, and addresses. Paste when you choose, or send compatible content straight from another app’s share sheet.

02 / USE IT

Right there when you need it.

Copy the original text, open a link, or send an address to Apple Maps. Read the whole thing with a tap.

03 / FORGET IT

Give it an end date.

Pick an hour, tonight, tomorrow, or your own time. Expired items leave the active inbox and are cleaned up when Ghost Paste next runs.

Here for a moment

A quick save.
A thoughtful goodbye.

Add an optional title and choose how long to keep something. The default is 24 hours, and you can change it in Settings.

Plans change

Need it longer?
Give it more time.

Add another hour or another day, or choose a new expiration. You stay in control. Routine deletion has a brief undo option, too.

Private by default

Your inbox lives on your device.

Ghost Paste has no accounts, servers, analytics, or tracking. Saved content stays in local storage shared by the app and its share extension. Device backups may include that data.
