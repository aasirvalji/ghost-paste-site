Try the principal flow

  1. Open Ghost Paste and tap Add item.
  2. Enter “Pick up flowers on the way home.” Add an optional title and choose 3 hours.
  3. Save the item. Tap Copy, or tap its title to read the full content.
  4. Choose Another hour or Set expiration from the item’s menu.
  5. Delete the item, then use Undo to restore it while the undo option is available.
  6. Open Settings to change the default expiration or view the privacy and support links.

Capture through the share sheet

In Safari or another app that shares text or web links, choose Share, then Ghost Paste. Look under More if needed. Review the captured content, choose an expiration, and tap Save. The extension also works while the main app is closed. Open Ghost Paste afterward to see the saved items.

Useful details for App Review

No sign-in, account, subscription, payment, or test credentials are required. The core app works offline. Opening external destinations or these hosted pages may require internet access. Paste is an explicit user action. Notifications are not implemented in version 1.0.

Expiration is based on actual saved dates; cleanup happens when the app next runs successfully. Saved content is local and shared with the extension through an App Group. The demonstration uses ordinary sample content, not private user data.

The video demonstrates functionality; it does not replace signed-device testing or Apple’s review of the submitted build.
